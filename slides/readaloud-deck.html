The minute that costs the most

A teacher sits with one child
while twenty seven others wait.

A running record is the most informative minute in elementary literacy assessment. Sixty seconds of reading, then four minutes of hand tallying every error. Three times a year, for every child.

The tallying is the part a machine should do. The judgement is not.

The control that matters

The override runs
in both directions.

Four filters sit in this engine and every one of them can only remove errors. If the only control a teacher had were the power to forgive, every adjustment the tool offered would push the score upward.

So a teacher can mark a flagged word correct, and mark a forgiven word as an error.

The teacher is the assessor. We are the stopwatch.

Our headline feature

A dialect layer, so a child who speaks African American English is not scored down for pronunciation.

Miscue analysis has done exactly that for decades. Speech recognisers make it worse: roughly double the word error rate for Black speakers across all five major commercial systems.

Labov & Baker 2010 · Charity et al. 2004 · Koenecke et al., PNAS 2020

The equity contribution is the architecture, not the rule table.

Aligning against a known hundred word passage collapses the recognition search space from roughly forty thousand candidates to a hundred. That absorbs most accent driven recognition error structurally, rather than by rule.

Ground truth is constructed, not annotated. We generate the spoken text from the reference by applying an explicit error script, so there is no labelling step to disagree with and no way to score our own homework.

Why false positives is the number we optimised

Telling a child who read correctly that they made a mistake is the worse failure.

Those two errors do not trade off evenly in a classroom. One costs a teacher thirty seconds of checking. The other costs a seven year old some part of their belief that they can read.

What it cannot do

We have never run this on a child’s voice.

Every evaluation clip is speech synthesis, one adult voice reading faster than the ninetieth percentile for grade six. A seven year old at sixty words a minute with real pauses will behave differently, and that is the largest unknown in the system.

Self correction recall is forty percent by construction. Published norms stop at grade six. It is a screening aid and a research prototype, not a validated instrument.

The path to one is an inter rater reliability study against certified human scorers, stratified by dialect. That is a semester, not a weekend.
